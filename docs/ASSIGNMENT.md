**3. What is the topic that you want to study using the AI setup?**

We study how a local AI model can help university students learn from their own course materials. Our project, Gen AI, runs a language model locally with Ollama and gives it "local knowledge": lecture PDFs, slides and notes that are extracted, indexed and searched on our own laptop. The tutor answers questions with citations to the exact page, generates study notes, and creates practice quizzes, in English and Thai, without sending any data to a cloud AI service.
